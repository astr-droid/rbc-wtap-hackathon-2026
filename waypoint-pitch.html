How we get there

It runs on tools RBC already has.

Working today

The flow you just saw, all three views.
Intake through an n8n webhook, with a fallback.
Calendar and chat steps mocked, and every call logged.

Pilot: one cohort, one term

Opens from Teams, Slack or Webex. No new login.
Outlook invites through Microsoft Graph, with Webex links.
Workday roster, read only. No new headcount.

After that

Every early-talent cohort.
The map follows a co-op into their new-grad role.
Mentor recruiting goes where the interest is.
Private answers and participation live in separate stores. Groups under five stay hidden. Nothing is written back to HR, and each check-in costs one short AI call.
